Research

Compositional Coordination Confidence for Multi-Agent LLM Systems

with Prof. Annie Qu · UCSB, 2026 – Present · in preparation · presenting at OEP research symposium Fall 2026

A method for halting multi-agent LLM pipelines mid-run with a formal guarantee. It attaches to whatever per-turn judge a system already uses, and controls how often it stops a pipeline that was actually working. Because the guarantee holds at any stopping time, operators can pick the tolerance after seeing the evidence rather than committing in advance. On two multi-agent benchmarks it holds false-alarm rate at 0.0% and 3.5% (α = 0.05) with detection at 8.1% and 16.2%; on long trajectories, early halting saves roughly 10% of per-trace tokens.

Instructing Efficient Early-phase Preclinical Alzheimer's Disease Trials

Gaona-Partida, Anuurad, Sequeira, Ragaine, Turner, Grill, Gillen · UC Irvine + collaborators, 2025 · co-authored poster at CTAD 2025 · manuscript in preparation

Constructed GLM, ridge, and CART classifiers identifying Alzheimer's progression biomarkers (pTau217, PACC, ADL) across 1,150+ A4 trial participants (0.835 AUC). Developed sample size reduction framework achieving 80% power with 188 vs. 500+ participants.
