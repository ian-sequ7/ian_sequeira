About

I'm a fourth-year Statistics & Data Science and Economics student at UC Santa Barbara. I like problems where you have to prove something works, not just show it does. That's what pulls me toward sequential and anytime-valid methods.

My senior thesis is with Prof. Annie Qu on formal guarantees for multi-agent LLM systems. Before that I worked on Alzheimer's biomarkers at UC Irvine's Summer Institute for Biostatistics with Prof. Dan Gillen. I also build data systems at Alida.

Outside of research you'll catch me DJing, in the gym, traveling, surfing, or working on my car. Usually some subset of these on any given week.

Research

Compositional Coordination Confidence for Multi-Agent LLM Systems

with Prof. Annie Qu · UCSB, 2026 – Present · targeting ICLR 2027 submission · presenting at OEP research symposium Fall 2026

Formulating the first formal definition of provable task-completion confidence for multi-agent LLM pipelines. Using e-value composition across DAG topologies with anytime-valid guarantees, validated over 1,642 annotated traces across 7 frameworks. Awarded SIOP Summer Research Scholarship (2026) by UCSB Office of Education Partnerships.

Instructing Efficient Early-phase Preclinical Alzheimer's Disease Trials

Gaona-Partida, Anuurad, Sequeira, Ragaine, Turner, Grill, Gillen · UC Irvine + collaborators, 2025 · co-authored poster at CTAD 2025 · manuscript in preparation

Constructed GLM, ridge, and CART classifiers identifying Alzheimer's progression biomarkers (pTau217, PACC, ADL) across 1,150+ A4 trial participants (0.835 AUC). Developed sample size reduction framework achieving 80% power with 188 vs. 500+ participants.
