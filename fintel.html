Fintel

Personal project · 2025 – Present · Python, Astro, React

I built Fintel because I wanted my own equity research tool that ran from the terminal, something I could iterate on faster than another dashboard. It reads market data from a handful of APIs, scores tickers on a small factor model, and pulls in the macro signals I actually check on a day-to-day basis.

Factor scoring

The scoring model is deliberately simple: momentum (25%), quality (25%), valuation (20%), growth (15%), analyst consensus (10%), and smart-money flows (5%). The point wasn't to invent a new factor. It was to see whether a legible, tunable stack could actually change what I looked at day-to-day. It does. Being able to change one weight and re-rank the universe in a few seconds is the whole game.

Smart-money tracking

Tracks 13F filings, insider Form 4 transactions, and Congressional trades. When insiders are buying or activist funds are taking positions, that gets weighted into the score. Mostly this is a filter for "worth reading more about," not a signal by itself.

Macro side

Monitors 22 economic indicators across 8 categories: credit spreads, housing, recession probability (Sahm rule), financial-stress indices, inflation, labor, valuations, currency dynamics. Each maps to sector-level impacts. This part started as a way to sanity-check whether news stories matched the underlying data, and stayed because it turned out to be more informative than the news.

Everything else

Daily briefings with the economic calendar and historical reaction patterns; portfolio analytics (paper trading, P&L, Sharpe, tax-loss harvesting); a TradingView hookup for technicals. All of it lives behind a CLI I can pipe into other tools.

Stack

Python 3.11 with async httpx, SQLite for persistence. Frontend companion in Astro 5 + React 19 + Tailwind 4.
