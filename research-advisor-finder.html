Research Advisor Finder

Personal project · Nov 2025 – Feb 2026 · Next.js, FastAPI, pgvector

This came out of my own PhD search. Skimming faculty pages was slow, and the existing tools were mostly ranked lists that didn't tell me why anyone was a match. I wanted something that read a CV or an interest paragraph and pointed at faculty whose actual published work overlapped, with an explanation I could read in thirty seconds.

It indexes 15,000+ faculty across 12 STEM fields at US R1 research universities. Live at research-advisor-finder.vercel.app.

Matching

The search is hybrid: vector similarity over paper embeddings, full-text over abstracts, and tag matching over declared areas. Each of these catches things the other two miss, so combining them recovers more real matches than any of them alone.

Per-match reasoning

Claude generates a per-match breakdown across three axes: Topic Alignment (do their areas overlap yours?), Paper Relevance (do their specific papers relate?), and Research Fit (is there a plausible collaboration?). The ranking arrives with reasoning rather than a bare score, which was the whole reason I built it.

Modes

Search takes a plain-English description or a CV upload. Compare lets you evaluate up to three faculty side-by-side. Explore is a guided mode for students who don't yet know what to search for.

Faculty profiles

Each professor page shows h-index, citation counts, top papers, and career-stage badges. Not because those metrics decide anything, but because they're the fastest way to orient before reading the papers themselves.

Stack

Frontend on Vercel (Next.js 15, React, Tailwind, shadcn/ui). Backend on Railway (FastAPI, PostgreSQL, pgvector). OpenAI embeddings for the vectors; Claude for the explanations.
